# STILLPOINT

A post-apocalyptic relic-wasteland bullet-hell roguelike built for iPhone (portrait one-thumb + Lift-to-Focus time-dilation).

## Features
- **Lift-to-Focus (`STILLPOINT`) + 5 Swappable Stillpoint Cores**: Drag anywhere to steer your scavenged hover-rig and graze enemy fire for Kinetic Charge; lift your thumb (or hold **Space** on desktop) to enter pinpoint bullet-time and trigger your equipped Core — **Kinetic Nova** (shock blast & knockback), **Phantom Blink** (forward warp + decoy detonation), **Gravity Siphon** (enemy vortex pull + shrapnel shatter), **Overclock** (2.3× fire rate & auto-crit), or **Razor Reflex** (execute slash + shield & haste stacks).
- **12 Dual-State Weapons + 12 Passives + 12 Catalyst Evolutions**: Every weapon shifts behavior between Normal Time and Stillpoint Slow-Mo — including **Cryo Ring** (chill → Stillpoint shatter) and **Swarm Missiles** (paint lock-ons in Stillpoint → release homing salvo on touch-down).
- **12 Wildcard Run Relics**: Build-breaking artifacts offered from Bosses and Relic Haulers (*Prism Split*, *Volatile Shrapnel*, *Chrono Overdrive*, *Blood Engine*, *Graze Surge*, *Ghost Step*, *Executioner*, *Scrap Harvest*, *Orbital Overdrive*, *Aegis Protocol*, *Double Tap*, *Static Harvest*).
- **5 Stackable Heat Mutators**: Toggle *Swarm +40%*, *Hyper Fire*, *Ironclad*, *Glass Edge*, and *Cursed Void* for +25% Shards each (up to +125%).
- **Momentum Surge, Grazing & Mid-Run Tactical Events**: Skim past enemy projectiles to recharge Focus and chain kills/grazes to ignite **SURGE ×4** (+32% fire rate & +18% movement), break **Resonance Shrines** for XP, Shards, and Magnets, intercept golden **Relic Haulers**, survive **Swarm Rings**, and dodge **Slag Barrages**.
- **Pick-10 Class Loadout Builder**: Unlock 18 build-defining modules across Weapons, Focus, Chassis, and negative-cost Risk Traits (*Stripped Hull*, *Overheated Core*, *Locked Draft*) that expand your 10-point budget up to 15 points, saved across three class presets (`A`, `B`, `C`).
- **Keys, Run-Earned Crates, Ships, Effects & Pattern Skins**: Earn **Salvage** and **Classified Crates** from strong runs, Bosses, Relic Haulers, and Trials, then buy universal **Crate Keys** with Shards to unbox 14 procedural pattern families with deterministic **Pattern Seeds (`#000–#999`)**, exact **Float Wear (`0.0000–0.9999` FN → BS)**, rare **`ST`** kill trackers, **Ship Chassis**, **Thruster Exhausts**, **Stillpoint Auras**, and **5→1 Trade-Up Contracts**. Duplicate Ship or Effect drops convert into bonus Shards.
- **High-Stakes Bullet-Hell Balance**: Steep earned XP scaling, armored multi-phase bosses with Stillpoint resistance (bypassable via *Sniper Lens*), lethal enemy fire, standing-still Focus drain, 4+4 build slots, and **Overclocked Draft Cards** with permanent Curses.
- **Cohesive Painted Wasteland Identity & Industrial UI**: 54 rendered sprites, seamless painterly top-down sector floors, wreckage props, and self-hosted *Chakra Petch* + *JetBrains Mono* chamfered gunmetal/brass hardware UI.
- **4 Wasteland Sectors, 14 Tactical Enemies & 4 Multi-Phase Bosses**: *01 ASH FLATS*, *02 SUNKEN METRO*, *03 SLAG FOUNDRY*, and *04 THE MONOLITH*, featuring Kinetic Leeches, Cluster Bombers, Shielded Phalanxes, Drone Carriers, and Endless Overdrive.
- **Reactive Synth Soundtrack + 1-Tap Mute**: Dynamic D-minor synthwave score with Stillpoint low-pass sweep, instant mute, and iOS ambient audio mixing (never interrupts Spotify/Apple Music).

## Installing on iPhone via Sideloadly
1. Download `STILLPOINT-unsigned.ipa` from the **Releases** page.
2. Open [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/) on Windows or macOS and connect your iPhone.
3. Drag `STILLPOINT-unsigned.ipa` into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, and click **Start**.
4. On iPhone: **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management** → trust your developer certificate, then launch **STILLPOINT**.
