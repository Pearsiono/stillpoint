STILLPOINT

Privacy Policy · Effective November 2026

STILLPOINT is an offline-first action roguelike. We respect your privacy and keep your game data strictly under your control.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) or third-party ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics, crash tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
  • No personal contact information, account registration, or location data.

Local & iCloud Save Storage

Your progression, ships, cores, loadouts, coatings, and settings are saved locally on your device. On App Store builds, if you are signed into iCloud on your device, STILLPOINT uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage to sync your save across your own Apple devices. We cannot access or read your iCloud data.

Apple Game Center

If you choose to sign into Apple Game Center, your high scores and trial achievements are submitted directly to Apple Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy. Game Center is completely optional.

Questions about privacy? Open an issue or reach out via the Support page.
