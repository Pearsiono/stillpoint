STILLPOINT

Privacy Policy · Effective November 2026

STILLPOINT is a free, offline-capable action roguelike. We do not collect personal accounts, emails, or location data.

Optional Rewarded Advertising (Google AdMob)

STILLPOINT never displays forced banners or interstitial ads. If you choose to tap Watch ad in the Armory for an optional Scanner Boost, STILLPOINT uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to serve a rewarded video ad.

  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, coarse diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on optional rewarded ads.
  • On iOS, you will see Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. You may decline tracking and still play the full game and earn optional rewarded boosts using non-personalized ads.
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Local & iCloud Save Storage

Your progression, ships, cores, loadouts, coatings, and settings are saved locally on your device. On App Store builds signed into iCloud, STILLPOINT uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage to sync your save across your own Apple devices. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Apple Game Center

If you choose to sign into Apple Game Center, your high scores and trial achievements are submitted directly to Apple Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy. Game Center is optional.

In-Game Patch Notes

STILLPOINT fetches a static read-only JSON file from GitHub Pages to display developer announcements and patch notes. No user identifiers are sent with this request.
