STILLPOINT

A portrait one-thumb bullet-hell roguelike built for iPhone. Hold to weave through storms of fire, lift right on the brink of incoming shots to unleash electric shockwaves, and bank Bullet Pressure without stalling your reactor.

12 ships, 9 Stillpoint Cores, Pick-10 buildcrafting loadouts, 4 wasteland sectors, weapon evolutions, and optional rewarded Scanner Boosts only (zero forced or banner ads).
