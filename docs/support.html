STILLPOINT

Player Support & Quick Guide

Controls & Core Mechanics

  • Move: Hold and drag anywhere on the screen. You can switch between Floating Stick and Direct 1:1 Drag inside Settings or the Pause menu.
  • Stillpoint: Lift your thumb to slow time and activate your equipped Core.
  • Brink Shockwave: Lift into Stillpoint right as an enemy bullet scrapes your hull to trigger an electric shockwave that pushes enemies back and refunds Focus.
  • Bullet Pressure & Reactor Stall: Holding Stillpoint near live bullets charges Overpressure for an amplified exit burst. Drain Focus to 0% and your reactor stalls for 2.4s, locking out slow-motion.
  • Evolutions: Level any weapon to LV 5 while holding its paired passive to unlock its Evolved form. Pause anytime to inspect evolution pairs.

Backing Up or Restoring Progress

Progress syncs automatically via iCloud on signed-in devices. You can also export or restore a manual SP1:... backup code inside Settings → Backup / Restore.
